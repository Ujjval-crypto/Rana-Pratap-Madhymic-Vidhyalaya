Who we are

Rooted in values, focused on the future

Rana Pratap Madhymic Vidhyalaya was founded with a simple belief: every child deserves an education that develops both ability and character.

Our school brings together academic rigour, creative exploration, physical well-being and a strong sense of community. We create an environment where students feel known, challenged and inspired to do their best.

  • Student-centred teaching
  • Respect, discipline and integrity
  • A safe and inclusive campus
